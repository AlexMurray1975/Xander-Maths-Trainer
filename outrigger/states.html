Big Ocean States

Small Island Developing States steward 30% of the world’s ocean through their exclusive economic zones, and receive eleven times less finance than other lower-middle-income countries. Outrigger invests where that gap is widest.

The eligible geography

Thirty-five states, three regions, one strategy

Outrigger focuses on 20 of the 38 UN SIDS Member States for the investment window. The remaining countries are addressed through the hub-and-spoke strategy and the Technical Assistance Facility. At least 80% of the fund is deployed into ODA-eligible developing countries, with a 15% floating allocation.
