The team

Meet the people running the fund

Simon Dent

Managing Director

Over 25 years of transactional experience in environmental finance and commodities. Simon began working in the blue economy in 2015 and launched one of the first ocean funds in 2018, deploying approximately $100m into blue economy projects in the Global South. He launched and ran the Althelia / Mirova Sustainable Ocean Fund from inception in 2016 until it was fully invested in 2023, and was previously a Managing Director at BNP Paribas in its commodity and carbon finance division.

Jeremy Milward

Managing Director

More than 25 years in business strategy, growth and value creation consulting, with extensive experience in due diligence and portfolio growth. Jeremy spent the last five years running his own firm, working closely with capital providers on due diligence cases and portfolio growth strategies. He previously founded and exited his own firm, ran UK & Ireland for Infosys Consulting, and worked for the World Bank (SEADF) in Bangladesh.

Emma Lear

ESG & Impact Director

Over 10 years as ESG lead in impact funds. Emma has created the impact analysis process and run impact assessments as part of an integrated fund due diligence approach, and leads Outrigger’s SIDS-tailored impact framework and its reporting against 14 indicators.

Abigail Mottur

Impact Finance Associate

Supporting origination, analysis and impact measurement across the portfolio and the Technical Assistance Facility.

Ines Henriques

Operations Director

Responsible for the fund’s operations, working with the third-party AIFM, service providers and the governance structures that sit around the investment process.

Governance

The Investment Committee

The Investment Committee assesses and ratifies all investment opportunities in coordination with the Expert Board, and provides oversight of all investment assets within the fund. It comprises up to six persons, supported by the investment teams.
