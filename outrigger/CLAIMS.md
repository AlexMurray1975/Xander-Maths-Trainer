# Claim substantiation register

Every quantitative and sustainability claim that appears on the website, with
where it appears, where it came from, and who needs to approve it.

This exists because Outrigger Impact is an Appointed Representative of Robert
Quinn Advisory LLP, and because the FCA's anti-greenwashing rule (ESG 4.3.1,
in force since 31 May 2024) requires sustainability references in a UK
financial promotion to be consistent with the product's actual characteristics
and to be fair, clear and not misleading. A register makes the principal's
approval exercise tractable, and makes it obvious what has to be re-checked
when a figure changes.

**Status column:** `SOURCED` — traced to a named source in the material
supplied. `VERIFY` — needs the original source located and dated before
publication. `COUNSEL` — needs legal or compliance sign-off on wording, not
just on the number.

Two source documents are referred to throughout:

- **PR26** — Outrigger Impact press release, *Outrigger Impact Fund I Reaches a
  First Close*, London, 28 July 2026.
- **DECK25** — Outrigger Impact Fund pre-marketing presentation, 2025,
  including the World Bank 2024 indicators reproduced in its appendix.

## 4 · Material NOT published on the website

None of the following appears anywhere on the site. It is provided to
professional investors on request, and is approved as a financial promotion
separately from the website.

Target returns (senior debt 5.5% gross IRR, equity 17.0% gross IRR) · tranche
sizing · junior tranche ≈30% of fund · management fee 2% / 1.85% · ticket size
US$2–10m · term and investment period · regional allocation percentages ·
investment window state list · indicative pipeline with instruments and ticket
sizes · investment models · capital structure.

Source for all of the above: DECK25. Status: COUNSEL — approve as part of the
investor materials, not as part of the website.

**Two items were changed on review advice and should not return in their
original form.**

*"A principal private debt guarantee is available"* has been deleted. It cannot
be published without naming the provider, the instrument covered, the
proportion, the conditions and exclusions, and whether it is committed or
merely contemplated.

*"Protected by the junior tranche"* was too absolute. Subordination reduces but
does not remove risk to senior capital: where losses exceed the junior tranche,
senior capital is exposed. Use *"benefiting from the structural subordination
of the junior tranche"* and state the limitation alongside it.

## 5 · Sector and market statistics

The six-sector market data — CAGRs, market sizes to 2030–2032, sector growth
rates — **has been removed from the site.** It was on the home page and is now
nowhere.

Two reasons. It read as a pitch deck embedded in a web page, and every figure
carried a substantiation and maintenance burden disproportionate to its
persuasive value: an LP does not need convincing that marine electrification is
a real market.

If any of it is reinstated, each figure needs a named source, a date and an
owner, and belongs in the investor materials rather than on the website. The
original figures are in DECK25 slide 7.

| Whether to cite a Luxembourg statutory provision at all | — | Luxembourg counsel |
| Press release "Important information", reproduced as issued | News article | Already approved 28 July 2026 |

**Two changes made on review advice:**

*"SFDR Article 8+"* is now *"classified under Article 8 of SFDR"*. "Article 8+"
is market shorthand, not a statutory classification, and risked implying an
official higher category.

*"pursuant to section 330 of the Investment Code"* has been **removed from the
site footer**. It looked anomalous beside otherwise precise UK FCA and MiFID II
language, and needs Luxembourg counsel to confirm the citation before it is
published. It remains in the press release, which is reproduced as issued.

## 7 · Standing instruction

Approval attaches to the site as approved, not to the disclaimer. Material
changes to claims, pipeline, named investors, target figures, photography,
logos or the public/restricted boundary go back through the approval process.

When any figure in this register changes, update it here first, then the site.
