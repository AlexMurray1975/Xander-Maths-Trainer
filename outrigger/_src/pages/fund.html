A fund built to make island economies investable

Outrigger Impact Fund I provides dedicated blended finance to projects in island states, encouraging entrepreneurship that generates market-based returns while enhancing ocean equity.

Strategy

Six things the fund does differently

  1. Originate and structure

    Build investable project opportunities in the islands and accelerate the delivery of impact investment capital and project execution — rather than screening a pipeline that does not yet exist.

  2. Hub and spoke, then aggregate

    Link and expand investment projects across islands and regions so that individually sub-scale opportunities are delivered at scale.

  3. Transfer network and knowledge

    Bring experience, relationships and innovative solutions from the team’s track record and from global markets into markets that rarely see them.

  4. Finance a unique geography, in full

    Provide finance to a set of ocean countries across the full spectrum of blue economy projects, and deliver incremental co-investment from public and private investors.

  5. Put impact first

    Demonstrate an impact-first approach tailored to the needs of SIDS, with ESG and impact KPIs embedded into the investment process.

  6. Run a TAF in step with the fund

    Structure a Technical Assistance Facility aligned with the investment window, providing grants to support investment readiness and to build capacity for early-stage projects with strong potential to scale.

Sustainable, resilient economies

SIDS create more sustainable and resilient economies, diversified within the blue economy rather than concentrated in one or two exposed sectors.

02

Investment models

Four models, chosen for replicability

Outrigger has shaped four investment models used to drive island investment, each designed so that a success in one island can be repeated in the next.

Model 01

Direct SME investment

Direct investments into scalable and growth-centred small to medium-sized enterprises.

Model 02

Island joint ventures

Leveraging blue business models and technology solutions to deliver systemic change to the islands, through structuring island-based joint ventures.

Model 03

Project finance

Focused project financing specifically for environmentally supporting infrastructure.

Model 04

Investable conservation

Investments into ocean conservation that is economically sustainable.

Capital structure

Blended finance, built to de-risk an unfamiliar geography

Junior equity of roughly 30% of the fund takes first loss, protecting senior equity and senior notes. A principal private debt guarantee is available, and ESG and impact risk is mitigated by a detailed environmental and social management system.

Junior tranche

Catalytic first loss

Supported by development finance institutions and philanthropic partners, absorbing first losses on behalf of the senior tranches. Cornerstone commitments from the Nordic Development Fund and the Luxembourg–European Investment Bank Climate Finance Platform.

Senior tranche

For private investors

Designed for private investors seeking both financial returns and measurable impact outcomes, protected by the junior tranche. A principal private debt guarantee is available.

Governance

Independent oversight at every stage

  • Investment Committee

    Independent members alongside Outrigger management. Assesses and ratifies all investment opportunities in coordination with the Expert Board, and provides oversight of all investment assets. Up to six persons, supported by the investment teams.

  • Expert Committee

    Sectoral and impact experts advising on technical and impact questions specific to each sector.

  • Advisory Board

    Nominated investors, giving limited partners a formal channel into the fund’s direction.

  • Grant Committee

    Key donors and Outrigger management, governing the Technical Assistance Facility’s grant-making.

  • Third-party AIFM

    Alter Domus Management Company S.A., a Luxembourg-based Alternative Investment Fund Manager, sits as observer.
