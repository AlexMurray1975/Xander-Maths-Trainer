Capital structure

Blended by construction

A junior equity first-loss tranche of roughly 30% of the fund protects senior equity and senior notes. A principal private debt guarantee is available, and ESG and impact risk is mitigated by a detailed environmental and social management system.

Senior notes$40mTarget 5.5% gross IRR.
Senior equity$30mTarget 17.0% gross IRR, protected by the junior tranche.
Junior equity — first loss$30mCatalytic capital from development finance institutions and philanthropy, absorbing first losses. Cornerstone commitments from the Nordic Development Fund and the Luxembourg–EIB Climate Finance Platform.
Outrigger Technical Assistance Facility$10mA separate UK company limited by guarantee. Grants and repayable loans of $50k–$250k. Projects may graduate to the fund.
