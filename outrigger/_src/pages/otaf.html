The Outrigger Technical Assistance Facility

Most island projects are not un-investable. They are un-ready. OTAF closes that distance — building the capacity, capability and ecosystem for blue economy SMEs across Small Island Developing States.

In brief

Designed in conjunction with the fund, and working in tandem with it

OTAF has been developed alongside the Outrigger Impact Fund and works with it to catalyse funding and investment in early-stage projects, helping develop social, economic and environmental resilience.

It is constituted separately, as a UK company limited by guarantee with its own Grant Committee of key donors and Outrigger management. It became operational in June 2025 and is now supporting projects across the Caribbean, the Indian Ocean and the Pacific. The facility is backed by UK International Development, the Ocean Risk and Resilience Action Alliance, the Global Environment Facility and the Nordic Development Fund. Projects supported by the facility may graduate into the fund.

What OTAF funds

Four workstreams

01

Investment readiness

Support for projects with strong investment potential that need targeted assistance to address the gaps preventing them from securing investment — whether those gaps are of maturity, financial, operational, impact-related or ESG-related.

02

Capacity building

Building capacity within SIDS: the technical capacity needed to develop Marine Spatial Plans and to develop and implement ocean stewardship policy, alongside expanding business capability within SMEs.

03

Ecosystem restoration & conservation

Restoration and conservation are vital to building environmental resilience to climate change. By improving ecosystem health, OTAF supports coastal livelihoods through fishing and eco-tourism, and strengthens natural coastal protection. The facility aims to enhance the protection and conservation of 10,000 hectares of coastal and marine areas.

04

Poverty alleviation

Strengthening SIDS economies drives resilience at two scales. At island scale, through businesses built on high environmental and social foundations with strategies for adapting to a changing climate. At individual scale, through access to climate-resilient jobs and livelihoods that bring regular income to poor and vulnerable communities.

How OTAF works

Early-stage blue economy projects and SMEs across Small Island Developing States, with strong potential to scale. The facility works across the same eligible geography as the fund, including the states addressed through the hub-and-spoke strategy rather than the initial investment window.

Grants and repayable loans of $50,000 to $250,000, directed at investment readiness, capacity and capability building, nature-based solutions, and ecosystem restoration and conservation.
